The problem

Why July bills keep getting worse.

Two things are happening at the same time, and both push your bill up.

01

Rates climb every year.

Southern California utilities have raised residential rates every year this decade. What you paid last July is not what you will pay this July.

02

Heat waves stretch your AC.

When the temperature climbs, your AC runs longer and your home crosses into the utility's highest pricing tier. Those tier rates apply to every kilowatt past the threshold.

03

There is no relief in sight.

Rate hikes get approved every cycle, and SoCal summers are not getting cooler. Without a fixed-rate solution, next July is likely to cost you more than this one.

The fix

Solar gives you a fixed-rate roof.

A solar system locks in your power cost the day it is switched on, and it produces the most on the same hot, sunny days your AC works hardest.

  • Lock in your power cost for 25+ years with a Tier-1 panel and inverter system.
  • Peak production on peak days — solar output rises with sun, right when you need it.
  • Net metering banks the surplus — overproduce by day, draw on credits at night.
  • Add a battery to ride through outages and shift away from utility peak-price hours.
