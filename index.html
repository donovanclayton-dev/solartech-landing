The problem

Every Month You Wait, Your Utility Wins.

San Diego utility rates have climbed 7–9% every year for the past decade. The real question is whether you can afford to keep paying a bill that goes up every year.

01

Rates climb every year.

San Diego homeowners are paying 40–60% more in electricity now than five years ago. Rate hikes get approved every cycle — without solar, that trend continues year after year.

02

Heat waves push you into the highest tier.

When the temperature climbs, your AC runs longer and your home crosses into the utility's highest pricing tier. Those penalty rates apply to every kilowatt past the threshold.

03

There is no good time to wait.

Every month you delay is another month you hand money to the utility. Solar locks in your rate the day it switches on — and San Diego summers are not getting any cooler.
